For hotels and tourist accommodation in Cyprus without a full licence

Nine documents. Three deadlines. One key board.

Under Law 110(I)/2026, an establishment without a licence from the Deputy Ministry of Tourism can keep operating only with an operating confirmation (βεβαίωση λειτουργίας), backed by nine documents from different authorities. Deadlines

The dates in the law

They apply to establishments that already have a building permit for hotel or tourist accommodation use.

  1. 31 Dec 2026

    Operating confirmation from the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, with the nine documents.

  2. 28 Feb 2027

    Fire safety certificate submitted. If not, operation ends immediately and the confirmation is cancelled.

  3. 31 Dec 2028

    From this date nobody may operate a hotel or tourist accommodation without an operating licence.

Source: Law 110(I)/2026 amending the Hotels and Tourist Establishments Law, Article 18 (cylaw.org).

Where things stand

About a quarter of operating hotels are fully licensed. The Deputy Minister of Tourism said on 17 November 2026 that no further extension is planned unless serious weaknesses in the licensing procedures are found, and that hotels missing the deadline will be reported.

A bill tabled on 22 October 2026 proposes to move the fire certificate deadline to 28 February 2029. It was discussed in the House committee on 17 November 2026 and has not been adopted. Until it is, the dates above apply, and the documents would remain required either way.

Sources: Cyprus Mail, 17 November 2026; Kiprinform and Politis, 30 October 2026.

Documents

The nine documents, Article 18(2)

α

Fire Service document confirming the application for a fire safety certificate (fire study by a private engineer and as-built drawings)

β

Health certificate or health confirmation

γ

Health certificate or confirmation for the swimming pool, where there is one

δ

Suitability certificate from the Electromechanical Services Department

ε

Lift inspection report without remarks

στ

Suitability confirmation for the liquid petroleum installation, or a declaration where none is needed

ζ

Periodic inspection report for the LPG installation or storage, or a declaration where none is needed

η

Letter or confirmation from the Department of Labour Inspection that a written risk assessment exists

θ

Copy of the first page of the building permit for hotel or tourist accommodation use

The confirmation can be revoked after an inspection if any of these documents has lapsed or no longer meets its conditions.

FAQ

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. The check is an indicative overview built from your answers and the published law. It is not legal advice and it does not replace your lawyer, engineer or architect.

What if the extension bill passes?

The check states the dates in force on the day it is prepared. The documents stay required under the current law and under the bill.

Do you need copies of our certificates?

No. The free check works from your answers. Do not upload documents with personal data.

Who is it for?

Owners, general managers and technical managers of hotels, hotel apartments and other tourist accommodation in Cyprus, and the consultants who help them.
