How it works

Three steps from phone to wardrobe.

  1. Profile

    Your sizes, your cut, your family in one place.

  2. Fitting room

    The outfit on your own photo, layer by layer.

  3. Buy only what fits

    Size advice for this exact piece, then straight to the shop.

What it does

Made for real bodies, not catalogue models.

Two sizes at once

Wear both L and XL? Pick both. The app remembers which one fit you in which brand.

Cut to your mood

Oversized or fitted. The outfit shows on your photo the way you'd actually wear it.

A memory for brands

Fit, too small, too big. Every bit of feedback makes the next suggestion better.

Petite to 6XL

Regular, Petite, Tall, Plus and Big & Tall. No limits on height or weight.

The whole family

Every screen you own

One account

You, your kids, one wardrobe.

Privacy from day one

Accounts from 16, children's profiles managed by a parent, and the app asks only for what changes your size or style. No one — adult or child — is ever photographed or stored undressed.

For brands.

For brands and shops

A customer who already knows what fits.

Sedne sends people to your shop who have already put the outfit together, seen it on themselves and picked the size that fits them in your brand. You sell; we brought them to you.

One order, step by step

A customer builds a jacket and trousers in Sedne, both in the size that fitted her in your brand last time. She taps Buy, lands on your site and orders. You pay a commission only on that order. No order, no cost.

Fewer returns

The customer orders the size that fits them in your brand, not a guess. Returns are the most expensive part of an order, and Sedne works on them at the moment of choice.

Whole outfits, not single pieces

People build outfits in Sedne. The jacket, the hoodie and the trousers go to the basket together, not one item at a time.

You pay only for sales

We work through your affiliate programme: no setup fee, a commission only on orders that came through Sedne.

A new channel for Slovakia and Czechia

Sedne is built for the Slovak and Czech market first, with family profiles and sizes from kids to 6XL. Your brand is there from day one.

How the partnership works

  1. You send a feed

    A product feed or access to your affiliate programme. Size charts help, but aren't required.

  2. Sedne shows your pieces to the right people

    Only to people they fit, in their size, inside a whole outfit, on their own photo.

  3. The customer buys from you

    On your site, at your price. You pay a commission only on that order.

Questions

When does it launch?

The app already runs as a test version. We're finishing the family version now and preparing the iPhone and Android release. People on the waitlist get the test version this year; the public release is planned for early 2027. The roadmap above is what we hold ourselves to.

Where will it run?

iPhone and Android first, then the web. One account everywhere.

What will it cost?

We're still deciding. People on the waitlist will know first.

What happens to my photos?

They're used only to show the outfit on you. No one — adult or child — is ever photographed or stored undressed.

Where do the clothes come from?

From shops you already know. You buy each piece directly from them, at their price.
