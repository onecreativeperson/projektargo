Why now

Before the first payment

In Program Slovensko the provider verifies the size of the enterprise before paying the first payment claim. The applicant declares its category in a sworn statement; the provider checks owners in public registers such as the business register, RPVS, the register of organisations and the register of financial statements.

Methodological guideline of the managing authority for Program Slovensko No. 18, effective 27 Jan 2025 (eurofondy.gov.sk)

A new category above SME

Since May 2025 the EU also defines small mid-caps: enterprises that are not SMEs, with fewer than 750 employees and turnover up to 150 million € or a balance sheet up to 129 million €. The SME definition itself did not change.

Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1099 of 21 May 2025 (antimon.gov.sk)

  • Linked

    Majority of votes, right to appoint the majority of the board, or dominant influence. Also via a natural person, if the companies work in the same or an adjacent market. Counted at 100 %.

  • Partner

    A stake of 25 % or more without control, directly upstream or downstream. Counted in proportion to the stake.

  • Outside

    Below 25 %, or certain exempt investors. Not counted.

A change of category takes effect only if the ceilings are crossed in two consecutive accounting periods.

Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC, Annex, Articles 2, 3, 4 and 6 (EUR-Lex)

Questions

Which registers do you use?

For Slovakia: the register of organisations of the Statistical Office (owners and deposits of limited companies) and the register of financial statements. For Czechia: ARES. Czech financial statements are not read automatically, so you enter those figures.

Does this replace the SME declaration?

No. You still sign the declaration. The preview is a working document that shows what the registers say and where they say nothing.

What about links through people?

Companies can be linked through a natural person if they work in the same or an adjacent market. Registers show only part of this, so the preview lists it as an open question.

What does it cost?

The preview is free. The paid version is early access only: you can register interest, there is no payment now.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is indicative and based on your answers and on public registers.
