For fuel suppliers regulated under EU ETS2

Your first verified ETS2 report is due on 30 April 2027.

If you release heating oil, LPG, diesel, petrol, gas or coal for consumption, every tonne you released for consumption in 2026 has to be in an emissions report checked by an accredited verifier. ETS2 Desk maps your fuel streams before the verifier does.

Why now

The deadlines are already running

ETS2 covers fuels for buildings, road transport and additional sectors. The monitoring part did not wait for the 2028 start.

1 Jan 2025

Permit and approved monitoring plan required.

2026

First year whose data must be verified by an accredited verifier.

30 Apr 2027

Verified emissions report for 2026 due.

2028

ETS2 becomes fully operational.

Sources: European Commission, ETS2 page (climate.ec.europa.eu); Council approval of the one-year postponement to 2028, 5 March 2026 (ICAP).

New since 6 November 2026

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2467 sets rules on financial compensation for final consumers of fuels and on allowances surrendered for emissions not covered by ETS2, such as fuel that ends up in ETS1 installations. If you deliver to such customers, you need evidence of end use.

Source: EUR-Lex, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2467 of 5 November 2026, OJ of 6 November 2026.

FAQ

Questions

Is this legal advice or verification?

No. The snapshot is an indicative overview built from your answers and public sources. It is not legal advice and it does not replace your accredited verifier or the competent authority.

Which countries do you cover?

Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, Hungary, Romania, Slovenia, Croatia and Bulgaria first. Other EU countries on request, using that country's published factors.

Do I need exact quantities?

No. Approximate 2026 quantities per fuel are enough for the snapshot. The paid pack works with your actual data.

Why bother if ETS2 starts in 2028?

The postponement moved the start of allowance obligations. Monitoring, permits and the verified report for 2026 still apply.
