Why now

The clock starts on the approval date.

Every fact below is taken from the official text or the source linked next to it.

Approval dayArt. 89(3)

Apply by the approval date

The application for authorisation or parallel mutual recognition must be submitted no later than the date of approval of the active substance. With several substances, the last one approved counts.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) No 528/2012, consolidated text

+180 / +365 daysArt. 89(3)(a)(b)

No application, no market

Without an application, the product may no longer be made available on the market 180 days after approval. Existing stocks may be used for up to 365 days after approval.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) No 528/2012

Since 1.9.2015Art. 95(2)

Your supplier must be listed

A product with a relevant substance may only be made available if the substance supplier or the product supplier is included in the list for the product type.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) No 528/2012

1 May 2028?Ethanol · PT1, PT2, PT4

Ethanol is on the way

In February 2026, ECHA’s Biocidal Products Committee supported approving ethanol for PT1, PT2 and PT4. The Commission draft notified to the WTO proposes approval from 1 May 2028 to 30 April 2038, with adoption planned for February 2027. It is a proposal, not adopted law.

Sources: REACH24H (updated 14 October 2026), Bisafe

FAQ

Questions

Do you prepare the dossier?

No. Studies, the dossier, letters of access and the submission stay with your consultant or lab. We show you where you stand and which route to discuss with them.

Is this legal or regulatory advice?

No. The Snapshot is an indicative overview based on your answers and public sources. Confirm decisions with the competent authority.

Is the ethanol date certain?

No. 1 May 2028 is the date proposed in the Commission draft. It is not adopted and can change. We check the current status before every Snapshot.

Which countries do you cover?

Any EU country, up to 3 per free Snapshot. National transitional regimes are stated only from an open official source, otherwise marked “check with the authority”.

What data do you need?

Company data only: products, substances, product types, countries and your supplier. No personal data beyond your work contact.
