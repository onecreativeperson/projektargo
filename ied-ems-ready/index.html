Revised Industrial Emissions Directive · Article 14a

Two deadlines on one chart: 2027 in force, 2030 proposed.

Directive (EU) 2024/1785 requires each IED installation to have an environmental management system (EMS), with a first external audit, by 1 July 2027. The Council’s position on the Environmental Omnibus would move this to 1 July 2030 and drop the mandatory audit, but it has not been adopted. We send you a free, indicative readiness snapshot against both, by e-mail within 2 working days.

What is fixed, and what is still moving

The law in force sets 1 July 2027. The Council has agreed its position for negotiations with the European Parliament, which has no position yet. Until Council and Parliament agree and the act is adopted, plan against the rule in force and keep the 2030 scenario in view.

Questions

Is the 1 July 2027 deadline still valid?

Yes, as of November 2026. Directive (EU) 2024/1785 is in force and Article 14a sets 1 July 2027. The Council’s position would move it to 1 July 2030, but that only applies once Council and Parliament agree and the act is adopted and published.

We already have ISO 14001 or EMAS. Is this relevant?

Likely much of the structure is there. Article 14a adds specific elements, such as the chemicals inventory, substitution analysis and indicators linked to BAT conclusions. The snapshot shows which elements your current system already covers.

What do you need from us?

Company and installation data only: country, main activity and how far your EMS is today. No personal data beyond your work contact, and no documents.

Is the snapshot legal advice or an audit?

No. It is an indicative orientation based on your answers and public sources. Please confirm your obligations with your permitting authority or a lawyer.
