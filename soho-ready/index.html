The changes

Why now

The main changes for clinics and banks, from official and professional sources.

Registration for every SoHO entity

Every organisation carrying out SoHO activities must register. Processing with storage, release, import or export requires authorisation as a SoHO establishment.

Reg. (EU) 2024/1938 · EUR-Lex

Transition from the old directive

Establishments already authorised under Directive 2004/23/EC are deemed authorised as SoHO establishments. The directive ceases to apply from 7 August 2027.

ESHRE factsheet · SoHO Coordination Board

New roles: releasing officer and physician

Every SoHO entity designates a responsible person. An establishment that releases SoHO also designates a releasing officer, and every establishment appoints a physician for donor eligibility, collection and allocation procedures. One qualified person may hold more than one role.

Reg. (EU) 2024/1938, Art. 36, 49, 50 · EUR-Lex

SoHO preparation authorisation

SoHO preparations may not be released without a SoHO preparation authorisation. Preparations from processes authorised under Directive 2004/23/EC before 7 August 2027 are deemed authorised. Annual activity data stay due by 30 June of the following year.

Reg. (EU) 2024/1938, Art. 38, 82 · ESHRE factsheet

FAQ

Questions

We are already authorised. Is this relevant?

Yes. Establishments authorised under Directive 2004/23/EC are deemed authorised, but the Regulation adds or changes obligations: registration as a SoHO entity, a releasing officer and a physician, SoHO preparation authorisation, traceability and coding. The report shows which of these you still need to check.

Is this legal advice?

No. The report is indicative orientation based on your answers and public sources. Please confirm requirements with your national competent authority or a qualified adviser.

Do I need to share patient or donor data?

No. The form asks only about your organisation. Please do not enter any patient or donor information.

Who is this for?

Private IVF and assisted reproduction clinics, sperm and egg banks, and tissue or cell banks in the EU.
