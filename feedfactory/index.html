Why now

What the ETIM organisations and the electrical distributors say.

Wholesalers rely on your data

ETIM UK and Ireland states that many distributors now require manufacturers to supply ETIM-classified product data. The Electrical Distributors' Association notes that wholesalers rely on manufacturers' product data for the content of their websites.

ETIM UK and Ireland, FAQs · EDA, ETIM UK and Ireland

An industry data pool built on ETIM

EDATA, the EDA's data pool, was set up with 25 founder members: 10 wholesalers and 15 manufacturers. Its technical attributes are structured under ETIM, alongside master data and digital assets such as images and data sheets.

ETIM UK and Ireland, EDATA announcement, April 2020

A new exchange format

ETIM xChange is ETIM International's JSON format for exchanging product data, released as the successor to its earlier BMEcat recommendation. Version 2.0 was officially released on 27 November 2025.

ETIM International, exchange format · xChange 2.0 release

The standard itself is open

The ETIM classification is free to use under the Open Data Commons Attribution Licence. The international English version is open to all; some national translations, including English (UK), are reserved for members of the national ETIM organisation.

ETIM International, licence info

Questions

Do I need to upload my catalogue?

No. For the free check you only describe 3 to 5 products in the form. Please do not send confidential price lists.

Is this an official ETIM certification?

No. Feed Factory is not affiliated with ETIM International or any national ETIM organisation. The check is an indicative match based on the public ETIM Viewer; please confirm the requirements with your wholesaler.

Which ETIM language do you use?

The international English version, which is open to all. If your wholesaler works with English (UK) or another national translation, class and feature codes stay the same, but names can differ.

Who is this for?

Small manufacturers of electrical, lighting, cable management, heating and similar building products, roughly 5 to 200 people, who sell through wholesalers or distributors and have no dedicated data team.
