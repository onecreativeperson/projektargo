The law

What you can claim, in two countries

Slovakia

Interest
ECB main refinancing rate on the first day of the calendar half-year + 8 percentage points; the rate is reset every half-year of the delay. Alternatively the creditor may claim the ECB rate on the first day of delay + 9 pp, fixed.
Compensation
€40 per claim that is the subject of a separate invoice, due by law once the invoice is overdue, regardless of the length of delay or the amount.
Time limit
4 years (general limitation period in commercial relations).
Commercial Code § 369, § 369c, § 397; Government Regulation 21/2013 Coll.; Supreme Court of the SR, decision R 11/2023 (2Obdo/59/2021); Ministry of Justice SR, rate table

Czech Republic

Interest
ČNB repo rate on the first day of the calendar half-year in which the delay began + 8 percentage points; fixed for the whole delay.
Compensation
At least CZK 1,200 for each claim between businesses (and for supplies to public contracting authorities).
Time limit
3 years (general limitation period).
Government Regulation 351/2013 Coll. § 2 and § 3; Civil Code 89/2012 Coll. § 629; ČNB repo rate history

EU Court of Justice, C-370/21 (1 Dec 2022): where one contract provides for periodic supplies, the minimum €40 is owed for each late payment.

FAQ

Questions finance teams ask

Is the audit really free?

Yes. One audit of your export, delivered by e-mail within 2 working days. No payment details, no obligation.

Will this damage relationships with key customers?

You decide whom to claim from. The audit shows the amounts per customer, so you can choose, for example, only former customers or chronic late payers.

Why two variants of the €40 compensation?

The Slovak Supreme Court (R 11/2023) grants €40 for each claim that is the subject of a separate invoice, provided the invoice is not split on purpose. The audit also shows the lower variant of €40 once per customer so you can see the range.

What if we agreed a different rate with a customer?

An agreed rate takes precedence. The audit uses the statutory rate and marks the customers for whom you said you have your own agreement.

Which formats work?

CSV, XLSX or XML exports from Pohoda, Money, iDoklad, Fakturoid, SuperFaktúra, KROS, ABRA, Helios, SAP or Excel. We need invoice number, customer, amount, currency, due date and payment date.
