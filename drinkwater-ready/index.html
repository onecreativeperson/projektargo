Why now

The rules change on one date. The transition depends on the country.

Every fact below is taken from the official text or the European Commission page linked under it.

31 December 2026

EU positive lists apply

Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/367 sets the European positive lists of starting substances, compositions and constituents. Article 4: it applies from 31 December 2026.

Source: EUR-Lex, Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/367
From 2027

EU certificate and marking

Compliant products are issued an EU certificate and a specific EU marking. A new product with no national certificate is assessed by a notified body; the manufacturer draws up a declaration of conformity and applies the marking.

Source: European Commission, Drinking Water Directive implementation
31 December 2032

National transition ends

An existing product can continue to be used in a specific Member State until 31 December 2032 if that state set criteria for a national conformity certificate valid on 31 December 2026. The transition works country by country.

Source: European Commission, Drinking Water Directive implementation (Q&A: working views of Commission services)
2028 and later

List entries expire

Entries in the positive lists carry expiry dates, for example 31 December 2028 or 31 December 2034. A material that is fine today may need a renewed entry later.

Source: EUR-Lex, Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/367, Annex I

FAQ

Questions

Do you certify products?

No. Certification is done by notified bodies. We sort your products and show what is missing, so you know what to send where.

Is this legal advice?

No. The Snapshot is an indicative overview based on your answers and public sources. Confirm decisions with your notified body or the competent authority.

Which countries do you cover?

Any EU country. The transition works per Member State, so the Snapshot lists each country of sale separately. National details we cannot confirm from an official source are marked “check with the authority”.

What data do you need?

Company data only: products, materials, countries of sale and approvals you hold. No personal data beyond your work contact.

How fast is it?

Within 2 working days after you send the form, by e-mail, in the language of the form.
