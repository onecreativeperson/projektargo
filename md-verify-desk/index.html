Why now

The register is live. The checks are yours.

Under Articles 13 and 14 of the Regulation, importers and distributors verify the CE marking, the declaration of conformity, labelling and UDI. The MDCG guidance adds that you should be able to demonstrate to the competent authority that these verifications were performed, through your internal records or procedures.

Published
27 Nov 2025

Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371 confirms that four EUDAMED modules are functional, including actor registration, UDI/device registration and notified bodies and certificates.

Source: Official Journal, L series 2025/2371

Mandatory
28 May 2026

These four modules became mandatory to use. Verifying that a manufacturer is identified and an authorised representative designated can be done via EUDAMED.

Sources: European Commission, EUDAMED overview · MDCG 2021-27 Rev.1

FAQ

Questions

Do you need any patient data?

No. The form asks only for company and product data. Please do not send any information about patients.

Does the Snapshot replace our own verification?

No. The obligations stay with you as importer or distributor. The Snapshot shows what the public register says and what is still open, so your own checks are faster. Physical checks, such as whether the importer details are on the label, still have to be done on the products you supply.

What if our price list has no UDI-DI?

We try to match by product name, manufacturer and reference number, and mark how confident the match is. With a UDI-DI or GTIN the match is much more reliable.

Is this a legal opinion?

No. It is an indicative check based on public register data on the day of the check. It is not a legal opinion and not a regulatory assessment of your products.
