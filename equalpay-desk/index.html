What applies in Greece now

Six fittings to check.

Summary of Law 5316/2026 as described in the Ministry of Labour circular 26923/07-10-2026 and practitioner guides (RSM Greece, include.org.gr). Point 06 is a Directive requirement that is also in the Cyprus draft bill.

  1. Pay before the interviewCandidates get the starting pay or its range within a reasonable time before the interview (or before the contract, if there is no interview).
  2. No pay-history questionsYou may not ask candidates about their pay in current or previous jobs.
  3. Answer pay requests within 2 monthsOn a written request, an employee gets in writing their own pay level and the average pay by sex for the same category.
  4. Tell staff every yearAll employees must be informed each year of their right to request this information.
  5. Objective, gender-neutral criteriaPay-setting criteria must be accessible to staff; with 50+ employees, pay progression criteria too.
  6. Gender-neutral ads and job titlesDirective (EU) 2023/970, Art. 5(3); included in the Cyprus draft bill.

FAQ

Before you start.

Does this apply to a small company?

In Greece, the transparency duties (pay before the interview, no pay-history questions, answering pay requests) apply to private employers regardless of size. Headcount matters for pay-gap reporting: 150+ employees from 2027, 100–149 from 2031, under 100 voluntary.

We are in Cyprus and the law is not passed. Why now?

The EU deadline was 7 June 2026. The Cyprus draft bill follows the same Directive, and the Labour Minister said in September 2026 that the first reporting deadline under the current draft is 6 June 2027. The snapshot checks you against the draft and marks what may still change.

Do you need salaries or employee names?

No. The form asks only about your company and its practices, plus job titles for the matrix. Please do not send personal data of employees.

Is this legal advice?

No. The snapshot is an indicative checklist based on public sources. For legal certainty, ask a lawyer or your labour advisor.
