Why now

What the directive asks of platforms, from official sources and published legal analysis.

Transparency on automated systems

Platforms must inform people working through them, their representatives and, on request, authorities about automated monitoring and decision-making systems.

Council of the EU · Mason Hayes & Curran

Human oversight

Decisions to restrict, suspend or terminate an account cannot be taken by an automated system. Platforms must regularly evaluate the impact of automated decisions.

Mason Hayes & Curran

Not only for employees

The safeguards on personal data and automated systems (Articles 7 to 11) protect people performing platform work, including those not classified as employees.

ECIPE · October 2026

Presumption of employment

The directive creates a rebuttable presumption of an employment relationship where facts indicate direction and control.

Mason Hayes & Curran

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. The snapshot is indicative orientation based on your answers and public sources. National transposition laws may add or change requirements, so please confirm with a qualified lawyer.

Do I need to share worker data?

No. The form asks only about your platform as a company. Please do not enter data about individual people.

Who is this for?

Small and mid-sized digital labour platforms working in the EU: delivery, home services, cleaning, care, tutoring, freelance marketplaces and staffing or shift apps.

Which obligations likely apply to you, a table of gaps with priorities, five next steps and links to the sources used. It arrives by e-mail within 2 working days.
