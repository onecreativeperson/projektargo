Top 5 risk areas

  1. Clinical notes that do not justify the procedure, referral or prescriptionHIGH
  2. Supporting document that does not match the declared procedureHIGH
  3. Possible duplicate billing: two claims for one visitCHECK
  4. Imaging and physiotherapy referrals well above the specialty averageCHECK
  5. Procedure codes (CPT) and diagnosis codes (ICD-10) that do not support each otherCHECK

Documentation checklist

  • Each visit note states the reason, the findings and why the procedure or referral was needed
  • The attached document belongs to the declared procedure and contains its results
  • Referral fields are filled in, not blank
  • Photos attached to claims are unique to each patient case

3 warning signs

Notes shorter than one line · the same attachment on more than one claim · a referral rate rising month after month.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to send patient data?

No. The free Snapshot uses only practice-level answers and totals. The form asks you not to enter names, ID numbers or diagnoses of individual patients.

Who prepares the Snapshot?

Štefan Toporcer, an independent product designer based in Slovakia, with the help of AI (Anthropic Claude), from your answers and public sources: published HIO decisions and the GESY codification rules. It is not connected to the HIO or GESY.

Is this legal advice?

No. The Snapshot is an indicative overview of risk areas. For a dispute or a pending HIO decision, speak to a lawyer.

What does it cost?

The Snapshot is free. The monthly Claim Guard is offered as early access at the prices above; nothing is charged now.

Does it work for physiotherapists, labs and imaging centres?

Yes. The risk areas are chosen for your provider type. Physiotherapy and imaging are among the first areas on which the HIO said its AI-supported checks will focus.
