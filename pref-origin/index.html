PEM · EU–UK · 2025/1728

Why now

The rules changed in the last two years. Long-term supplier’s declarations made with the old rules in mind may no longer say enough.

  1. 1 Jan 2025

    The revised Pan-Euro-Mediterranean (PEM) Convention came into effect, in parallel with the old one.

    Access2Markets, European Commission
  2. 8 Aug 2025

    Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1728: suppliers must state the legal framework they used to determine origin in their supplier’s declarations. Applies from 1 Jan 2025.

    EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2025/1728
  3. 1 Jan 2026

    Between the EU and PEM partners only the revised rules apply, except for parties that have not ratified them. Morocco, Egypt, Palestine and Tunisia export to the EU under transitional rules.

    Access2Markets, European Commission

How it works

  1. 01 · IN

    Send 1 product

    Product, tariff code if you know it, ex-works price and the materials with value, origin and whether you hold a supplier’s declaration. Two short form pages.

  2. 02 · CALC

    We calculate

    We look up the rule for your heading in the agreement text, add up non-originating materials and check the alternatives and tolerance the rule allows.

  3. 03 · OUT

    You get the result

    Within 2 working days by e-mail: meets / does not meet / data missing, the price threshold and what to check.

Questions

Is the result binding?

No. It is an indicative calculation from the data you send and from public sources. It does not replace binding origin information from customs or advice from a customs specialist. Responsibility for a declaration of origin stays with the person who issues it.

Which agreements do you check?

The revised PEM rules (for example Switzerland, Norway, the Western Balkans, Ukraine), the EU–UK agreement and other EU agreements such as Canada, Japan or South Korea. In the free check we do not cover textiles (chapters 50–63) or Türkiye; there the result will say “data missing”.

What if we do not know the tariff code?

Describe the product. We state the heading we assumed and mark it as something to verify.
