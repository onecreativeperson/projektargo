Regulation (EU) 2025/2365 · applies from 17 Dec 2027

Every pellet that leaves your site now needs a plan.

From 17 December 2027, each site that handles 5 tonnes or more of plastic pellets a year needs a risk management plan, a self-declaration of conformity and yearly loss records. Find out in 2 working days what that means for your plant.

01 · Why now

The dates are already in the Official Journal.

The Regulation was adopted on 12 November 2025. Most duties start on 17 December 2027; certification of larger sites follows by company size.

  1. 12 Nov 2025

    Regulation (EU) 2025/2365 adopted.

  2. 17 Dec 2027

    Rules apply: plan per site, notification with self-declaration, loss records, staff training. Large companies certify sites of 1,500 t or more.

  3. 17 Dec 2028

    Medium-sized companies certify sites of 1,500 t or more.

  4. 17 Dec 2030

    Small companies certify sites of 1,500 t or more.

  5. Every 5 years

    Sites under 1,500 t and micro companies renew the plan and self-declaration.

Who is in scope

5 t or moreCompanies that handled at least 5 tonnes of pellets in the EU in the previous calendar year (Art. 1).
Each siteA risk management plan per installation, the listed equipment and procedures in place, and the plan notified to the authority with a self-declaration (Art. 5).
RecordsYearly estimated losses and total quantities handled, kept for 5 years (Art. 5).
TransportEU and non-EU carriers and cleaning installations for containers and tanks are covered too (Art. 1).
PenaltiesFor the most serious breaches by companies, Member States must allow fines of at least 3 % of annual EU turnover (Art. 20).

Source: Regulation (EU) 2025/2365, Art. 1, 5, 6, 20, 26 and Annex I, EUR-Lex. Read the Regulation

02 · The plan

What Annex I asks you to write down

Ten items for every installation. The Snapshot tells you which ones you already have.

  1. A site plan
  2. Tonnes of pellets handled per year
  3. Spill locations, rated high or low risk
  4. Where losses could leave the site and which areas they reach
  5. Handling operations where spills can happen
  6. Yearly estimate of spills and losses
  7. Activities of suppliers, contractors and off-site storage
  8. A staff role that records, investigates and reports spills
  9. Packaging and equipment to prevent, contain and clean up
  10. Procedures to prevent, contain and clean up

06 · FAQ

Short answers

We handle less than 5 tonnes a year. Does this apply?

The Regulation covers companies that handled 5 tonnes or more in the EU in the previous calendar year. Below that, the Snapshot will say so and stop there.

We already follow Operation Clean Sweep. Is that enough?

Operation Clean Sweep is a voluntary industry programme. The Regulation adds legal duties: a plan per site, notification to the authority, a self-declaration and records. The Snapshot shows what you already cover and what is still open.

Do we need a certificate?

Only sites handling 1,500 tonnes or more a year at large, medium or small companies need certification, with deadlines in 2027, 2028 and 2030. Smaller sites and micro companies use the self-declaration.

What do you need from us?

Only the answers in the form. No documents, no site visit, no call.

Is the Snapshot legal advice?

No. It is an indicative overview based on the official text of the Regulation. Your competent authority and a qualified adviser have the final word.
