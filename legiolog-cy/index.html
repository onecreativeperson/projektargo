Log 02 · Public record

Why now

What the authorities have said publicly in 2026.

  1. Cyprus Mail, 8 July 2026

    A named person and a written assessment

    The Department of Labour Inspection said employers and building managers should appoint a person responsible for Legionella risk management, carry out regular written risk assessments and have a prevention and maintenance plan in place.

  2. Cyprus Mail, 8 July 2026

    Temperatures, flushing, cleaning

    Hot water above 50 °C and cold water below 25 °C where possible, regular flushing of rarely used taps and showers, cleaning and disinfection of tanks, pipes, shower heads, filters and cooling towers, and regular spa maintenance.

  3. Cyprus Mail, 8 July 2026

    Records you can show

    Keep records of inspections, cleaning, temperature checks and water analyses. Particular care is needed in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centres, care homes, hotels and other tourist accommodation.

  4. OEB, 9 April 2026

    Inspections in hotels this season

    From April to October 2026 the Department runs a health and safety inspection campaign in hotels, restaurants and ready-meal premises, based on the written risk assessment.

Log 04 · Questions

Questions

Q1Is this legal or technical advice?

No. The snapshot is indicative orientation based on your answers and public sources. It does not replace an on-site risk assessment by a competent person or laboratory testing.

Q2Do you need guest or patient data?

No. The form asks only about the building and how you manage its water systems. Please do not enter any personal or health data about guests, patients or residents.

Q3Is there a deadline or a fine?

The July 2026 announcement did not give deadlines or fines. It presents Legionella prevention as part of employers' health and safety obligations.

Q4Who is this for?

General, technical and quality managers of hotels and hotel apartments, private clinics and care homes in Cyprus.
