Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 · Bird & Bird, EU AML Package

One EU rulebook from July 2027

Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 is the new EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation, part of the EU AML package. It applies from 10 July 2027.

Cyprus Property News, 21 Jan 2026 · CBN, 20 Jan 2026

A new supervisor is proposed

Today estate agents are supervised for AML by the Estate Agents Registration Council. A draft amendment published for public consultation in January 2026 proposes to transfer this supervision to the Tax Department.

Cyprus Property News, 21 Jan 2026

A wider scope is proposed

Under the draft, supervision would cover all professionals who buy or sell property, or who represent buyers or sellers on a professional basis, unless another authority already supervises them.

CBN, 20 Jan 2026

Status: check before you plan

The draft was open for comments until 14 February 2026. Your snapshot checks the latest public status of the Cyprus amendment at the time of your request.
