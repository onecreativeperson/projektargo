Cyprus · Business Support Center register

Know your 70:30 position before the register checks it.

From 1 January 2027 every company on the BSC register (formerly the Register of Companies with Foreign Interests) must file an annual employment report. The 70:30 ratio is checked seven years after registration – for 2022 registrations that is 2029. Send 8 headcount figures, no names, and get a free BSC Compliance Snapshot by e-mail within 2 working days.

Why now

The rules changed on 5 November

The Business Support Center has revised the framework for companies with foreign interests. Registration now also brings recurring duties – and a clear way to be removed from the register.

  1. 27 Oct 2026

    The Council of Ministers approves the revised registration framework.

  2. 5 Nov 2026

    BSC announces it: the register is renamed and a removal mechanism is set out.

  3. 1 Dec 2026

    New registration applications are assessed under the revised framework.

  4. 31 Dec 2026

    Last day of the €2,000 renewal rule for existing key personnel. The highly paid threshold is €2,500 gross per month.

  5. 1 Jan 2027

    Annual detailed employment report becomes mandatory (Annex I template). BSC announces deadline and procedure in January 2027.

  6. Registration + 7 years

    70:30 compliance check – also for companies already registered. 2022 → 2029.

Sources: Business Support Center announcement of 5 November 2026 and Guide to Registration in the BSC Register of 4 November 2026 (gov.cy/bsc); Civil Registry and Migration Department, “Legislation and policy” and “Procedure for application submission” (gov.cy/mip-md).

FAQ

Questions

Is this legal or immigration advice?

No. The snapshot is an indicative calculation from the figures you provide and the public BSC and Migration Department guidance. For decisions on your company, consult a lawyer or immigration adviser.

Do you need employee names or passport data?

No. The form asks only for totals. Please do not send any personal data of employees.

Does the 70:30 check apply to companies registered before the change?

Yes. The BSC announcement says the check seven years after registration also applies to companies already registered. Its guide gives 2029 as the check year for 2022 registrations.

When is the annual employment report due?

BSC states that the deadline, reporting period and procedure will be announced in January 2027. The snapshot lists what the report must contain, so you can prepare the data now.

Can I get the snapshot in Greek?

Yes. Use the Greek page (ΕΛ in the menu); answers sent through the Greek form are answered in Greek.
