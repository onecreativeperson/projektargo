Why it exists

A lap time survives about ten minutes

You drive, you get a printout or a photo of the monitor, and a week later you have no idea whether you were faster than last time. Every track runs its own system and none of them knows about the others.

Paper gets lost

The printout goes in a pocket and ends up in the washing machine.

Every track on its own

Prešov knows nothing about Bratislava. On a new circuit you start from zero.

No sense of progress

Without history, 42.8 seconds is just a number. It cannot tell you whether that is good.

Track not listed? You add it yourself — anywhere in the world. You can log times on it straight away. It stays marked as unverified until we confirm it or the operator takes it over. So a holiday in Cyprus counts towards your profile too.
How it works

Three ways a lap time reaches the app

Whichever is possible at that track is used. The goal is that in the end you type nothing at all.

The track sends times itself best

When a track is connected, the time lands in your profile seconds after the session. You do nothing. This is what we want at every track.

You photograph the results sheet

When the track is not connected, you photograph the printout or the monitor. The app reads names and times from the photo and shows you what it recognised so you can correct it.

You type it in

Best lap, date and kart number. Ten seconds, and it works everywhere.

What Hotlap does not do: it does not measure time. The track's timing system does that. Hotlap takes the result and gives it meaning over time.

What earns you points

Levels

Every 500 XP is one level. Seven of them, from Rookie to Legend.

Score is not the same as XP

Score starts at 1200 and moves with how you drove against others on the same layout. It is the only number comparable across tracks — lap times are not, because 40 seconds in a short indoor hall is not 40 seconds on a long outdoor circuit.

Teams and duels

Three friends against three others

A team is 2 to 8 drivers. You create one, send your friends a five-character code, and you see each other's times. Then you can challenge another team.

IN A TEAM YOU SEE

Which kart is quick

Log the kart number with your session and Hotlap shows which karts at that track actually go and which do not.

Team against team

You pick the opponent, the period, and whether it runs on one specific track or on all of them. The second option is the interesting one — and the one that needs explaining.

How you compete across different tracks

Seconds from different circuits cannot be compared. So seconds are not what gets compared — the gap to the fastest is. On each layout and class, the fastest time in the duel scores 1.000 and everyone else is measured against it.

Example · a duel across two tracks
Marek — Prešov41.6501.000
Jana — Prešov43.7301.050
Peter — Bratislava38.2001.000
Tomáš — Bratislava40.1101.050
Marek and Peter drove completely different circuits and both score 1.000 — each was the fastest in the duel at his own track. Jana and Tomáš both lost 5 % and score 1.050. Lower wins.

Four rules that keep it fair

  • Only as many drivers count as the smaller team fielded. If three of you drove and two of them did, the best two from each side count. A bigger team cannot win on numbers.
  • Each driver contributes their best ratio, not every session. Ten slow sessions neither hurt nor help your result.
  • Times from a rebuilt layout do not count. When a track changes its circuit, old times move to history and the duel runs on the current configuration only.
  • Only the same layout, kart class and version are compared. Junior and adult karts are not the same leaderboard.

How a track connects

Write to us

One piece of information is enough: which timing system you run.

We connect it

A webhook, an API, or a small program that watches your export folder. The last option works on any system that can save a file.

You take over the page

A verification link goes to an address on the track's own domain. Whoever opens it becomes the manager.

What we take from your system: the name from the results sheet, the kart number and the lap times. Nothing else. No phone numbers, no e-mails, no payment data — even if the system sends them, the API discards them.
Straight up

What Hotlap cannot do yet

It is work in progress and there is no point pretending otherwise.

  • No track is connected yet. Automatic delivery of times is designed and ready, but the first track is still to be found.
  • Reading from photos is not running yet. It switches on when the app is connected to a database.
  • The app does not know your average lap. It stores the best lap and the lap count, not the total session time. An average would be invented.
  • Circuit shapes are not drawn automatically. GPS does not work indoors. We trace the layout by hand from the track's own plan.
